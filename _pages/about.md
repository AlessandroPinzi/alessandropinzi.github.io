Currently, I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics and Computer Science at University Luigi Bocconi. My reasearch interests are at the intersection of mathematical analysis and probability theory, in particular in optimal transport, metric analysis and gradient flows.

Feel free to contact me by email: [alessandro.pinzi@phd.unibocconi.it](mailto:alessandro.pinzi@phd.unibocconi.it).
